# Google Stock Market Analysis

## Project Overview

This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of Google's stock market data from 2004 to 2024. The analysis focuses on stock price trends, trading volume patterns, correlation analysis, moving averages, and long-term growth insights.

## Objectives

* Analyze Google's historical stock performance
* Study stock price growth over time
* Examine trading volume trends
* Perform correlation analysis between stock features
* Analyze moving averages and market trends
* Generate business and investment insights

## Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Google Colab

## Dataset Summary

* Company: Google (GOOGL)
* Analysis Period: 2004 – 2024
* Total Trading Days Analyzed: 5,118
* Features:

  * Open
  * High
  * Low
  * Close
  * Volume

## Data Cleaning

* Checked for missing values
* Zero missing values found
* Converted Date column to proper datetime format
* Sorted data chronologically from oldest to newest
* Prepared dataset for analysis and visualization

## Exploratory Data Analysis

The project includes:

* Stock Price Trend Analysis
* Trading Volume Analysis
* Correlation Heatmap
* Moving Average Analysis (MA50 & MA200)
* Long-Term Growth Analysis
* Historical Market Performance Visualization

## Key Insights

### Stock Price Growth

* Initial stock price in 2004: Approximately $2.50
* Stock price in 2024: Approximately $197
* Total growth over 20 years: Approximately 7800%

### Trading Volume Analysis

* Highest trading activity occurred between 2004 and 2008
* Trading volume gradually decreased as the stock matured
* Early investors demonstrated the highest trading activity

### Correlation Analysis

* Open, High, Low, and Close prices showed strong positive correlation
* Trading volume showed a negative correlation with stock prices
* Higher stock prices were generally associated with lower trading volumes

### Moving Average Analysis

* MA50 remained above MA200 for extended periods, indicating a bullish trend
* The 2020 COVID-19 market crash was clearly visible
* Strong recovery occurred after the 2020 market decline
* Long-term market trend remained positive

## Results

* Google demonstrated exceptional long-term stock market performance
* Consistent growth was observed throughout the analysis period
* Strong recovery from market downturns highlighted business resilience
* Technical indicators suggested a continuing upward trend

## Conclusion

Google has been one of the best-performing technology stocks over the last two decades. The analysis revealed approximately 7800% growth between 2004 and 2024, demonstrating exceptional long-term performance. Moving average analysis confirmed a strong bullish trend, while the stock successfully recovered from the 2020 market downturn. Overall, the data indicates consistent long-term growth, strong market resilience, and excellent historical investment performance.
